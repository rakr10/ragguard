# Customer Support and Accessibility

This describes support at the fictional Northbridge Bank.

## Opening hours

Phone support is open Monday to Friday from 8am to 8pm and on Saturday from 9am to 5pm. Phone support is closed on Sunday.

## Extra support

Our dedicated support line for customers who need extra help is 0800 000 5678.

You can ask for documents in braille, large print or audio format.

A text relay service is available for customers who are deaf or hard of hearing.
