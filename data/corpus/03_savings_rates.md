# Savings Accounts and Rates

Rates are shown for the fictional Northbridge Bank and are used for demonstration only.

## Easy Access Saver

The Easy Access Saver pays 3.10% AER variable. The minimum deposit is £1. You can make unlimited withdrawals.

## 1-Year Fixed Saver

The 1-Year Fixed Saver pays 4.25% AER fixed for the term. The minimum deposit is £500. You cannot make withdrawals during the term.

## Cash ISA

The Cash ISA pays 3.60% AER variable. The annual allowance is £20,000. We accept transfers in from other ISA providers.

## Regular Saver

The Regular Saver pays 5.00% AER fixed for 12 months. You can pay in between £25 and £250 each month.
