# Personal Current Account Fees

Northbridge Bank is a fictional bank created for this demonstration project. Nothing in these documents describes a real institution.

## Monthly fees

The Northbridge Everyday account has a monthly fee of £0.

The Northbridge Plus account has a monthly fee of £12 and includes mobile phone insurance and European travel insurance.

The Northbridge Premier account has a monthly fee of £25 and includes worldwide family travel insurance and breakdown cover.

## Everyday banking charges

Cash withdrawals at UK ATMs are free of charge.

The first replacement debit card in any 12 month period is free. Each further replacement debit card costs £5.

Paper statements cost £2 per statement on the Everyday account. Paper statements are free on the Plus and Premier accounts.

There is no charge for a returned direct debit.
