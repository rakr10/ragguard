# Overdraft Policy for Personal Accounts

This policy applies to personal current accounts only. Northbridge Bank is a fictional bank created for this demonstration project.

## Arranged overdrafts

An arranged overdraft is available to customers aged 18 or over, subject to a credit assessment.

The interest rate on an arranged overdraft is 39.9% EAR variable.

Plus and Premier customers receive the first £200 of an arranged overdraft interest free. Everyday customers do not receive an interest free amount.

We review your overdraft limit every 12 months.

## Unarranged overdrafts

An unarranged overdraft costs £5 per day, capped at £20 per month.

We will send you a text message if a payment would take you into an unarranged overdraft, so that you have time to pay money in.
