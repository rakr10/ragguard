# Residential Mortgages

This policy describes lending at the fictional Northbridge Bank and is used for demonstration only.

## Eligibility

The minimum deposit is 5% of the property value. The maximum loan to income is 4.5 times your annual income.

The mortgage term can be between 5 and 35 years.

## Fixed rates

The 2-year fixed rate is 4.19%. The 5-year fixed rate is 4.39%.

A product fee of £999 applies to all fixed rate mortgages. You can add the product fee to your loan.

## Early repayment charges

The early repayment charge is 3% of the amount repaid in the first year of a fixed rate period, 2% in the second year, and 1% in years three to five.

## Overpayments

You can overpay up to 10% of your outstanding balance each year without charge.

A mortgage offer is valid for 6 months.
