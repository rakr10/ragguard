# Cards and Fraud Protection

Details below describe the fictional Northbridge Bank and are used for demonstration only.

## Lost or stolen cards

You can report a lost or stolen card at any time of day or night on 0800 000 1234, or freeze your card instantly in the mobile app.

## Unauthorised transactions

If a transaction was not authorised by you, we refund it within 1 business day.

We will never ask for your full PIN or your online banking password by phone, email or text message.

We send a text message alert when we detect a suspicious transaction. If you reply N to that message, we block the card.

## Limits

The contactless limit is £100 per transaction. After £300 of cumulative contactless spending you must enter your PIN.

The daily ATM withdrawal limit is £500. For Premier customers the daily ATM withdrawal limit is £750.
