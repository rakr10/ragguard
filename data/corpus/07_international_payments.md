# International Payments and Spending Abroad

Charges below describe the fictional Northbridge Bank and are used for demonstration only.

## Sending money abroad

Payments in euros to SEPA countries have a fee of £0 and arrive within 1 business day.

Payments in other currencies have a fee of £9.50 and arrive within 2 to 4 business days.

## Spending abroad

There is no fee for card purchases made abroad.

Cash withdrawals in a currency other than sterling carry a fee of 2.99% of the amount withdrawn.
