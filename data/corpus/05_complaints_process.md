# Making a Complaint

This describes the complaints process at the fictional Northbridge Bank.

## How to complain

You can make a complaint by phone, through the mobile app, by post or at a branch.

## What happens next

We send you a written acknowledgement within 3 business days of receiving your complaint.

We will send you a final response within 8 weeks.

## If you are not satisfied

If you are not happy with our final response, you can refer your complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service within 6 months of the date of our final response.
